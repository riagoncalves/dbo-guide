## Map Revealers

Most of the world map is hidden at first. **Map Revealers** are devices placed around each zone: right click one to uncover its section of the map.

## Popo Stones

Popo Stones are statues topped with a gem bearing Mr. Popo's face, placed in towns and quest hubs.

- Interact with a stone to **bind** your return point to it.
- The **Invoke Popo** skill (10 second cast, 1 hour cooldown) returns you to your bound stone.
- When you are defeated, you revive at your bound stone.

## Yardrat Teleportation

Yardrat **Teleportation Service** workers stand next to their energy machines in every main village and at other spots around the world.

1. Visit a Yardrat once to register that location.
2. At any other Yardrat, right click the worker, choose Teleport and pick a registered destination on the world map.
3. Pay the fee. The price grows with the distance.

## Bus

Buses follow set routes and are the fastest way to travel along them, but they cannot leave their path.

## Vehicles

Personal vehicles are faster than walking and can go anywhere. No licence required.

- Rent them from vehicle merchants or buy them from the cash shop.
- Right click the vehicle in your inventory to board. Move with `W` `A` `S` `D`.
- Dismount with the button on the vehicle interface or with `Esc`. Entering combat dismounts you immediately.
- Fuel gives a temporary speed boost, and some vehicles are faster than others by default.
- The rental timer starts at purchase and keeps running while you are offline. When it reaches zero you can renew or discard the vehicle.

## Flight

After the [flight quests](quests/flight.md), double-tap `Space` to fly freely over the terrain.
