# Adult & Master Class
---

Every character starts the game as a child. At **level 30** a quest line at Korin Tower turns you into an adult, gives you your first [HTB](character/skills.md) and lets you pick one of the two Master Classes of your class.

| Stage | Requirement | Result |
| - | - | - |
| Adult quest | Level 30 | Adult body (hair style, hair colour and skin colour are kept), higher base stats and basic values, first HTB |
| Master Class choice | Adult quest completed | Choose one of two Master Class weapons from Korin |
| Master Class specialization | Weapon chosen | Master Class skills unlocked and the new sub weapon can be equipped |

!> The Master Class choice is permanent. Decide before talking to Korin, and reset your skill points before level 31 (see [Beginner Basics](getting-started/basics.md)).

## Adult Quest

You can do the first steps before level 30, but the chain can only be finished at level 30. If a step seems to be missing, you probably completed it earlier.

1. In **Korin Forest**, find and destroy an **Item Box** (a wooden crate). There are around ten spawn points across the map and the most used one is at **Pasta Tower**. If the crate is gone, wait for the respawn, change channel or try another spot.
2. Loot the **Korin Tribe's Spell**, right click it in your inventory and accept the quest.
3. The quest leads to the NPC **Hada**, near the Elder Residence teleporter. Turn it in and accept the follow-up. It shows no `X` on the map yet, which is normal.
4. Across from Hada (not right next to him), the NPC **Aizi** has a single quest. Accept it.
5. Aizi's quest leads to **Velmar** near City Gateway. She asks you to kill **10 Wild Boar Guerrillas** just outside the town. Only the boars carrying **staves** count, not the ones with swords.
6. Turn it in. The next step sends you to **Gila**, at the top of the Great Tree.
7. Gila sends you to **Sheila**, the gate keeper at the base of Korin Tower. Turn in the quest and talk to her again to be teleported to the top.
8. Talk to **Korin** and accept his quest. He places the **Super Divine Water** (it looks like a tea kettle) in your inventory.
9. Right click the Super Divine Water to summon **four Transparent Korins**, which appear two at a time. Defeat all four. Fighting from the edge of the platform lets you pull them one at a time.
10. Turn in the quest. You become an adult and receive your first HTB.

## Master Class Quest

1. Talk to Korin again and pick the **third option**. He shows two weapons, one for each Master Class of your class.
2. Choose one. Korin gives you the item; right click it to start the class quest.
3. The quest takes place in another race's starting zone. Open the map there and follow the `X`.

    | Your race | Quest zone |
    | - | - |
    | Human | Majin zone |
    | Namekian | Human zone |
    | Majin | Namekian zone |

4. The quest giver there gives a unique quest that rewards your Master Class weapon. You cannot equip it yet.
5. He then sends you to a special arena to defeat a powerful enemy, which is different for each class.
6. Turn in the quest. You now have your Master Class, can equip the weapon and can learn the Master Class skills.
