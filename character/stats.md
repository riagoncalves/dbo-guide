# Stats
---

Open the character window with `C` to see your stats. Six base stats feed every derived value on the sheet.

## Base Stats

| Stat | Main effect | Secondary effect |
| - | - | - |
| **Strength** (STR) | Physical Attack | – |
| **Constitution** (CON) | Max LP | LP recovery |
| **Focus** (FOC) | Hit Rate | Status success rate, Energy Attack, Energy Critical Rate |
| **Dexterity** (DEX) | Dodge Rate | Defense (block) rate, Physical Attack, Physical Critical Rate |
| **Soul** (SOL) | Energy Attack | – |
| **Energy** (ENG) | Max EP | EP recovery |

### Value per Point
The exact amount gained per point changes with race and class. The ranges below were measured by players.

| Stat | Gain per point |
| - | - |
| Strength | **1.1 – 1.4** Physical Attack |
| Soul | **1.1 – 1.4** Energy Attack |
| Dexterity | **5** Dodge Rate and **0.5** Physical Attack per point <br> **1** Physical Critical Rate every **7** points |
| Focus | **10** Hit Rate and **0.5** Energy Attack per point <br> **1** Energy Critical Rate every **7** points |
| Constitution | **75 – 105** LP, plus LP regeneration |
| Energy | **40 – 50** EP, plus EP regeneration |

Dexterity also raises physical critical damage, resistance rate and guard block rate, and Focus raises energy critical damage and status success rate. Those bonuses are not shown on the character sheet.

## Derived Stats

| Stat | What it does |
| - | - |
| Physical Attack / Energy Attack | Base damage of physical skills and basic attacks, and of energy skills |
| Physical Defense / Energy Defense | Reduces damage of the matching type |
| Hit Rate | Chance for attacks to land, checked against the target's Dodge Rate |
| Dodge Rate | Chance to avoid an attack entirely |
| Physical / Energy Critical Rate | Chance for an attack of that type to be a critical hit |
| Success Rate | Chance to land status effects such as stun, confusion or paralysis |
| Resistance Rate | Chance to resist those status effects |
| Defense Rate | Chance to block an attack automatically and take reduced damage. It also lowers the chance of being knocked down and raises the chance to counter while guarding. It does not help against debuffs. |

## Power Level
The Scouter shows a **power level** for every player and monster. It is calculated from all stats, with LP weighing the most, so treat it as a rough measure of how dangerous a target is.

## Growth

- Every level grants **1 Skill Point** (SP).
- Completing the [Adult quest](character/adult-and-master-class.md) at level 30 raises your base stats and basic values such as attack range.
- The [Dragon King buffs](dragon-balls/overview.md) wished from Shenron raise one base stat by a percentage for a limited time.
