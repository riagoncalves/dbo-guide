# Ultimate Dungeons (UDs)
---

In Age 1000 evil organizations took over several places and turned them into secret bases, known as **Ultimate Dungeons**. They can be entered alone or with a party of up to **5 players**, and give experience, quest rewards and valuable equipment.

## Rules

- Only members of the same party share an instance.
- A defeated player returns to their Popo Stone unless a teammate revives them.
- A player kicked from the party is moved outside the dungeon.
- The party leader sets the **difficulty** and can **reset** the dungeon after it is cleared to run it again. Both options are in the party menu.
- Quests inside a dungeon show no circle or `X` on the map.

Players also call these dungeons by older nicknames: UD1 is the Red Pants or "Tao Pai Pai" dungeon, UD2 is "Dr. Gero's" dungeon and UD3 is the museum.

## UD1 – Red Ribbon Underground Ruins

The former headquarters of the Red Ribbon Army in Korin Forest, now held by the Red Pants Army. It contains wolf pens and a robot repair bay.

- **Enemies:** Helmet sappers, bomb guards, rat infantry with mallets, medics, patrol robots, boar sentries, bear guerrillas, bulls, brainwashed alien trainees
- **Boss:** **Android 9**, a giant android built in the image of Commander Red and one of the leaders of the Red Pants Army
- **Quests:** RR Headquarters Map, Red Ribbon Army ID Update (5/5), Trophy of Red Pants
- Some bulls use area attacks that can defeat a low level character in one hit, so stay behind the tank.
- Hard mode can drop Legendary gloves and staves.

## UD2 – Red Pants Military Robotics

A Red Pants robot facility in Westland, populated by Android 19000 models and other machines based on Dr. Gero's designs. Its quests open at level 38.

## UD3 – Paella's Memorial

A museum in Mushroom Rocks North. Its first floor is a popular place for parties to level between 45 and 52. Many enemies apply bleeding.

## UD4 – Pilaf's Castle

Pilaf's old castle in Mushroom Rocks South, occupied by remnants of Frieza's army who turned it into a robot factory.

| Floor | Content | Boss |
| - | - | - |
| 1st | Robot assembly warehouse with excavator and utility robots | Iron Fist Muggley |
| 2nd | Control room | Chief Programmer Laster |
| 3rd | Kiri energy equipment | – |

- **Enemies:** Barbarian raiders, watchmen and infantry, guardian Pilaf machines, robot manufacturers, Kettlean officers, guard unit warriors, burning turrets
- **Other bosses:** Stone Fist Burt in the hangar, and Burakin

## UD5 – Oil & Gas Station

The level 54 dungeon of Yahhoi East. It has no Hard mode.

## UD6 – Chocolay Tower

A tower in Porunga Rocks South grown from Ajisa trees by the Time Breakers for **Chocolay**, the dark counterpart of Korin, in exchange for cultivating **Dark Senzu Beans**.

| Floor | Purpose | Boss |
| - | - | - |
| Ground | Ajisa tree cultivation | – |
| 1st | Growing Dark Senzu and extracting kiri | Chocolay (powers up into White Chocolay and summons flame flowers) |
| 2nd | Refining the kiri | Mr. Poko Poko |
| 3rd | Kiri storage | Gamelan, Naraka and the Dark Namek elders |

- **Enemies:** Black Waterway guards, healers and Poko masters, hydroponic Saibamen, alligators, Dark Senzu creatures, Narak masters (several are Ultra rank and use area attacks)
- **Guardians:** Ongde, Novocode, Moog
- Gamelan summons dark dragons and shadow copies of himself.
