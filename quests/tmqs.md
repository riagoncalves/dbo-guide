# Time Machine Quests (TMQs)
---

The Time Breakers, led by Mira and Towa, travel into the past to distort history. In a Time Machine Quest your party boards a time machine with Time Patrol Trunks and his robot **GBT-HOPE** and protects a key moment of the Dragon Ball story.

TMQs are repeatable party instances, available from **level 25**.

## How to Enter

1. Get the **concentrated liquid fuel** for the TMQ you want. Each TMQ has an unlock quest: accept it, defeat 30 monsters around your own level and turn it in to receive the fuel crystal.
2. Form a party of up to **5 players**.
3. The **party leader** talks to **GBT-HOPE** at the time machine and starts it with the fuel. Every party member is pulled in automatically.
4. Choose the difficulty before starting. Harder modes give better experience, upgrade stones and equipment.

## Rules

- A TMQ is a chain of **stages**. Each stage must be cleared before the next one opens.
- Every stage has its own **time limit**.
- The run fails if the time runs out or if the whole party is defeated.
- Story cutscenes play between stages.

## Walkthroughs

### TMQ1 – A Tense Smile on the Farm
The Time Patrol is ambushed on arrival while Raditz lands and heads off to find Goku. Chase the Time Breakers to the farmer's town, where remnants of Frieza's army are arming a bomb, and hold them off until it is disabled. Their leader flees to a frozen lake surrounded by mountains, where the final fight takes place.

- **Enemies:** Blaster aliens, Saibamen slaves, flying and snake Saibas, electric orb robots. The pale blue snake Saiba casts an area paralysis that can be interrupted with a stun. Saibamen near it self destruct.
- **Boss:** Android 8000 MX

### TMQ2 – The Journey Begins
You arrive on an island and swim to Kame House. The Time Breakers jam the Dragon Radar so Goku and Piccolo cannot track Raditz. Defend Kame House, take the submarine to the pirate cave and destroy the jamming devices, then return to the landing site and stall Raditz while Piccolo charges the Special Beam Cannon.

- **Enemies:** Melee aliens, rotor robots, purple crab Saibas, a pirate robot
- **Sub bosses:** Android 8000 Mk. II
- **Boss:** Raditz

### TMQ3 – The Nightmare
A Masked Man orders cannons built to shoot Piccolo out of the sky. Destroy them, then protect Gohan from brainwashed dinosaurs while he survives in the wild. After a time jump the Masked Man brainwashes Gohan in his Great Ape form: drive the Masked Man off and destroy the device while fighting the Great Ape.

- **Enemies:** Anti-air missile pods, blaster aliens, brainwashed mammoths and dinosaurs
- **Bosses:** Android 8000 (twice), Masked Man, Great Ape Gohan

### TMQ4 – Saiyan Invasion
Stall Nappa so that Gohan and Krillin survive until Goku arrives. While Goku and Vegeta fight elsewhere, destroy the **6 generators** powering a Time Breaker device. Then protect Gohan and Krillin and keep Great Ape Vegeta busy so Yajirobe can cut his tail. On the way back to the time machine the Masked Man attacks, and Mira appears for the finale.

- **Enemies:** Tosterian engineers and medics, Reginean soldiers, Kettlean blitz units, brainwashed Saibamen, freeze generators
- **Bosses:** Nappa, Great Ape Vegeta, Vegeta, Masked Man, Mira

### TMQ5 – Dark Cloud over Planet Namek
Towa takes control of Frieza's officers. Defeat Cui and Dodoria, stop the Time Breakers from stealing the Dragon Balls Vegeta has hidden and make sure they are in place when he returns, then help Vegeta against Zarbon.

- **Enemies:** Frieza soldiers, hovercraft
- **Bosses:** Cui, Dodoria, Zarbon

### TMQ6 – Namek's Fate
The Ginyu Force lands on Namek and Towa wants them for her experiments. Defeat Recoome and protect Vegeta, keep the Time Breakers from killing the Namekian frogs that Goku needs to trick Ginyu, then defeat Jeice and Ginyu in Goku's body.

- **Enemies:** Giant frogs
- **Bosses:** Recoome, Burter, Jeice, Guldo, Ginyu (in Goku's body), Frog Ginyu

### TMQ7 – The Strongest Enemies
Towa collects Nail's blood after his fight with Frieza, and the Time Breakers steal the piece of Frieza's tail cut off by Krillin and hide in a cave. Stop them from using it. Towa then enlarges the frog that holds Ginyu and turns it on the party.

- **Enemies:** Mira's scouting and security robots, Kettlean destroyers, fighters, medics and war lords, Reginean attackers, brainwashed guards, Android 8000 Mk. II
- **Bosses:** Tick, Tack, Toku, Mega Ginyu Frog

## Tips

- Bring a full party with a healer and a tank. TMQs are tuned for groups.
- In TMQ4, the soldiers around the generators keep respawning until the generators are destroyed. Parties use this to farm experience within the stage timer before finishing the objective.
- Hard mode of TMQ1 is a well known source of upgrade stones and Legendary armor for the 25–30 range.
