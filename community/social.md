# Party, Friends & Mail
---

## Party

A party holds up to **5 players**. Members appear on the left side of the screen.

### Inviting
- Right click a player and choose **Invite to Party**, or select a friend in the friend list (`L`) and use the party invite button. Both players must be on the **same channel**.
- A party name is 2 to 8 letters long. If you invite without naming it, the party is called "unnamed".

### Leader Options
| Option | Choices |
| - | - |
| Zeni distribution | Whoever picks it up, or random |
| Item distribution | Whoever picks it up, random, or to the player who dealt the most damage |
| Dungeon | Reset, Normal or Hard difficulty |
| Members | Kick a player, transfer leadership |
| Target marks | Number monsters or players so the whole party sees the same marks |

If the leader leaves, a random member becomes the new leader. Selecting a party member and pressing `F` attacks that member's target, and the party window shows the aggro list of the monster you are fighting.

## Friends

Open the friend list with `L`.

- Add a player by typing their name. You are notified when a friend logs in.
- From the list you can whisper, invite to a party, send mail, block or remove a player.
- Blocking a player hides everything they say in every chat channel.
- You can also whisper with `/w name`.

## Mail

Mailboxes are found in towns all over the world.

- A letter can carry a message, **one item** and Zeni to any character on the server. Postage depends on the contents.
- To attach an item, left click it and place it in the empty slot of the mail window.
- The Zeni box has two modes: **Send** delivers Zeni to the recipient, and **Request** asks the recipient to pay that amount to receive the attached item.
- Check the recipient's name carefully. A typo that matches another character delivers the letter to them.
- Read incoming requests before accepting them, since accepting a request pays the sender.
- Letters expire after a set time and are deleted or returned. Tick **Lock** on a letter to stop the timer.
- The mailbox holds **30** letters. When it is full you cannot see new mail until you delete some.
