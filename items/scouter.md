# Scouter
---

![](../_images/items/scouter.png) The Scouter is one of the most useful tools in the game. Its first quest, given right at the start, asks you to scan an NPC.

## Using the Scouter

| Key | Function |
| - | - |
| `T` | Opens the scan menu |
| `U` | Opens the Scouter window to insert and remove chips |

- Scanning a player, monster or NPC shows its **power level**, a number calculated from all of its stats with LP weighing the most.
- With the right chips the scan menu gains extra options, such as scanning the surroundings for quests.

## Chips

![](../_images/items/scouter-chip.png) Chips add functions or stats to the Scouter.

- A Scouter has a limited number of chip slots and a maximum **power capacity**. Every chip consumes some of that capacity, and the total cannot be exceeded.
- Better Scouters have a higher capacity.
- Scouters and the basic chips are sold by the Scouter merchant, a small blue robot found in towns.

| Chip | Effect | Where to get it |
| - | - | - |
| Quest search chip | Adds a second scan option that finds nearby quests, and marks scanned monsters needed for your quests with an orange balloon | Early quest (level 2–3). Also sold on the second tab of the Scouter merchant |
| Dragon Ball chip | Shows Dragon Ball carriers on the minimap during the [Dragon Ball Hunt](dragon-balls/overview.md) | First wish to Shenron at level 20 |
| Equipment chip | Lets you inspect the equipment of other players | – |
| Stat chips (they look like red clocks) | Add LP and other stats to your character | – |

?> The chip system was reworked when the level cap was raised to 70, shortly before the official servers closed in 2013. The description above covers the original system.

## Other Sources of Scouters

Scouters can also be [crafted](items/crafting.md) and bought with Mudosa Points from the [Budokai](pvp/budokai.md) merchants.
