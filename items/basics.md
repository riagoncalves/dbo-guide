# Item Basics
---

## Rarity

Equipment comes in five rarities. The rarer the item, the better its bonus effects.

| Rarity | Name colour |
| - | - |
| Common | White |
| Superior | Blue |
| Excellent | Yellow |
| Rare | Red |
| Legendary | Purple |

To see what a bonus effect does, equip the item and watch the change in the character window (`C`).

## Equipment Slots

| Group | Pieces |
| - | - |
| Weapons | Main weapon (gloves or staff) and the sub weapon of your Master Class |
| Armor | Jacket, trousers, boots |
| Accessories | Necklace, two earrings, two rings |
| Scouter | The [Scouter](items/scouter.md) and its chips |
| Dogi | Cosmetic outfit with its own [attributes](character/dogi-attributes.md) |

Weapons and armor each have a [battle attribute](character/attributes.md) and can be [upgraded](items/upgrading.md) to +15.

## Merchants

Merchants sell basic equipment, potions and food, and buy anything you do not need. They also offer three services:

| Service | Description |
| - | - |
| Repair | Restores the durability of one item |
| Repair all | Restores the durability of everything you have equipped |
| Identify | Reveals an unidentified `?` item |

`Ctrl` + right click buys a stack of 20. Besides normal merchants there are vending machines, capsule machines that give a random item for coins or points, and special merchants that only accept event currencies.

## Food and Potions

- **Potions** restore LP or EP instantly.
- **Food and drinks** restore LP and EP over time while your character is sitting (`X`).

## Weight

Every equipped item has a weight. If the total exceeds your capacity, shown by the green gauge next to your portrait, your stats are penalized.

## Selling to Players

- **Trade:** right click a player, or click their name plate, and choose Trade. Once both sides confirm, the trade is final.
- **Private shop:** open it from the Action Skills tab of the skill window. Place items and prices, name the shop and start selling. A yellow icon on an item means the seller accepts bargaining, which opens a private negotiation window.
- **Auction House:** in the south-east corner of West City. Treasure boxes, upgrade stones and Rare or Legendary items you cannot use sell well even at low levels. Right click an item with the search box selected and press search to check its going price.
- **Mail:** mailboxes send a letter with one item and Zeni to any character on the server. See [Community](community/social.md).
