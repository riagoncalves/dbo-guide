# Crafting
---

In Age 995 Capsule Corporation founded the **HoiPoi Capsule Mix Association (CMA)** to spread hybrid capsule technology. Its mixing machines let any player craft equipment from materials.

## What Can Be Crafted

- Main weapons (gloves and staves)
- Sub weapons (claws, sticks, swords, masks and the rest)
- Jackets, trousers and boots
- Necklaces, earrings and rings
- Scouters

## HoiPoi Machines

| Machine | Where |
| - | - |
| Crafting Master's machine | Next to the **Crafting Masters** in major towns and cities |
| Portable HoiPoi Machine | A Dinocap item that lets you craft anywhere. Close the crafting window when you are done. |

## Craft Level

- You start at **craft level 1**, which allows only the most basic weapons and armor.
- Every craft gives **Craft EXP**.
- Every **5 craft levels** unlock a higher tier of equipment.
- Your craft level and EXP bar are in the **Craft** tab of the character window (`C`).

## Recipes

- Crafting Masters sell the basic recipes. Rare recipes come from event NPCs.
- A recipe can only be used when your craft level is equal to or higher than the recipe level.
- Each recipe is learned **once** and stays in your crafting list permanently. Buying a duplicate is wasted Zeni, so sell it back or discard it.

## Results

Each craft can produce the standard version of the item, usually **Excellent**, or a better version, usually **Rare**, with higher base stats. Both results give the same Craft EXP.

## First Steps

An early tutorial quest introduces crafting: the crafting NPC, a beaver dressed as a doctor standing next to a blender, first gives you a chicken, a green pot and a carrot, then asks you to mix them into an item.
