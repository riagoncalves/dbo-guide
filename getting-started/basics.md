## LP, EP and RP

| Bar | Meaning |
| - | - |
| **LP** (Life Points) | Your health. At 0 LP you faint and revive at your bound Popo Stone, unless a teammate revives you. |
| **EP** (Energy Points) | Spent by skills and by Dash. Without EP you cannot use skills. |
| **RP** (Rage Points) | Fills when you take hits or when you charge with `Z`. A full bar becomes an **RP ball**, which powers up a skill. See [Skills, RP & HTB](character/skills.md). |

Food and drinks restore LP and EP while sitting, and potions restore them instantly. Use both generously while levelling.

The small green gauge near your portrait is your **carry weight**: every equipped item has a weight, and going over your capacity applies penalties to your stats.

## Finding Your Quests

- Quests are the main source of experience. Clear an area's quests before moving to the next hub.
- Open the map with `M`: red circles and `X` marks show the quest objectives. Hovering a circle shows the exact monster or object you need.
- Insert the **quest search chip** (given by a quest around level 2–3, or sold by the Scouter merchant) into your [Scouter](items/scouter.md). The scan menu (`T`) then gains a second option that scans the surroundings for quests.
- Scanning a monster with the chip installed shows whether it is needed for one of your quests.
- Some objectives are items dropped by a monster, so not every kill counts.

### Quests With No Map Marker
A handful of early quests (up to around level 25) have no circle or `X`. They are tutorials that ask you to perform an action once:

| Tutorial | What to do |
| - | - |
| Teleport | Use a teleport NPC at least once |
| Duel | Challenge another player to a Free Battle |
| Popo | Use your return skill (the Popo head in the skill window) |
| Upgrade | [Upgrade](items/upgrading.md) any item |
| Private shop | Open a private shop from the Action Skills tab |
| Party | Create a party, even an empty one |
| Arena | Queue for a [Ranked Battle](pvp/budokai.md) at the machines in a main town and finish the match |
| Capsule machine | Spend Mudosa Points on the capsule machine next to the arena NPCs |
| Crafting | Mix an item at the [crafting](items/crafting.md) NPC with the materials he gives you |
| Mail | Send a letter from a mailbox |
| Identify | Identify a `?` item at a merchant |

If a quest is still missing a marker, it most likely takes place inside a dungeon.

## Level Milestones

| Level | Unlock |
| - | - |
| 4 | [TLQ1](quests/tlqs.md) – learn **Power Up** (RP charge) |
| 5–20 | [Dragon Ball quests](dragon-balls/overview.md) – one ball at a time from your race's starting zone |
| 9 | [TLQ2](quests/tlqs.md) – learn **Guard** |
| 14 | [TLQ3](quests/tlqs.md) – learn **Dash** |
| 15 | First part of the [flight quests](quests/flight.md) |
| 20 | First wish to Shenron and the Dragon Ball chip for the [Dragon Ball Hunt](dragon-balls/overview.md) |
| 22 | [UD1](dungeons/ultimate-dungeons.md) – Red Ribbon Underground Ruins |
| 25 | [TMQ1](quests/tmqs.md) and the second part of the flight quests |
| 29 and below | Child Division of the [Budokai](pvp/budokai.md) |
| 30 | [Adult quest, Master Class](character/adult-and-master-class.md), first HTB and permanent flight. A guild can be founded from this level. |
| 45 | Dragon Ball Scramble |
| Every 10 levels | One extra RP ball slot |

!> Experiment with your skill points as much as you like up to level 30. From level 31 a skill reset costs tens of millions of Zeni or a cash item, so reset and settle on your Master Class build before then.
